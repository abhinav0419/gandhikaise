Gandhi, kahan se?(Gandhi — but from where?)

Four generations. Three countries. One surname — inherited, adopted, and married into along the way. This is a documented family tree, not a claim that anyone's name is fake. Circular marks next to a name are quick reference initials, colour-coded by family branch.

Feroze Gandhi

Journalist and Member of Parliament. Married Indira Nehru in 1942.

GHANDY GANDHI

Born Feroze Jehangir Ghandy, 12 September 1912, in Bombay. Biographies generally use the Ghandy spelling for his birth and early life, and the Gandhi spelling from his adult and political life onward. Historians differ on exactly why the spelling shifted — some link it to his closeness with the freedom movement, others describe it simply as a family variant already in use. No source claims the two spellings refer to different people.

His parents
Jehangir Faredoon Ghandy & Ratimai Commissariat

A Parsi (Zoroastrian) family from Bombay. Jehangir was a marine engineer. Feroze was the youngest of five children.

The claim: a viral story — repeated by some politicians — holds that Feroze's "real" surname was Khan, and that he was secretly Muslim.

  • His documented parents are Jehangir Faredoon Ghandy and Ratimai Commissariat — both Parsi family names.
  • He married Indira Nehru in 1942 in a Hindu ceremony.
  • On his death in 1960 he was cremated, and his ashes interred at the Parsi cemetery in Allahabad — a Zoroastrian practice, not an Islamic one.

Multiple independent fact-checks have examined and rejected the "Khan" claim. It appears to be a political rumor, not a documented fact.

No blood relation

Gandhi is a common surname in Gujarat, and this family is not related to Mohandas K. (Mahatma Gandhi) by blood. Feroze is widely reported to have adopted the spelling in admiration of him during the independence struggle, though accounts of the exact story vary.

Paternal grandmother
Indira Gandhi (née Nehru)
1917 – 1984

Prime Minister of India. Daughter of Jawaharlal Nehru and Kamala Nehru. Took the Gandhi surname on marrying Feroze in 1942, and kept it for the rest of her life and career.

Her side, one generation further

Jawaharlal Nehru (1889–1964), independent India's first Prime Minister, was the son of Motilal Nehru and Swarup Rani Nehru of Allahabad.

🇮🇹 Meanwhile, in Italy
Sonia Gandhi
b. 1946

Born Antonia Edvige Albina Maino in northern Italy (sources vary on the order of her given names). Known growing up as Sonia Maino.

She met Rajiv Gandhi in the late 1960s while he was a student in Cambridge, England. They married in New Delhi in 1968, and she took the name Sonia Gandhi. Separately, a widely circulated claim about her working as a "bar dancer" before marriage has been debunked — she has said she worked as a waitress while studying.

Why "Gandhi" and not "Maino"?

A name is a convention, not a blood test

Whose surname a child or spouse takes is a social and legal custom, not a biological rule. Rahul's father's family contributed the surname Gandhi; that convention doesn't erase his mother's own family name, Maino, or her identity — it's simply the more common pattern in most (not all) of the world.
